Your First Webpage – Step by Step

No experience needed. An AI writes the code for you – this guide walks you through everything else, until your page is online and you can share the link.

0 of 9 steps done
Part 1 · Make your page
📝

Plan your pageabout 5 minutes

›

Before anything else, decide what your page should show. It's like writing a shopping list before going to the supermarket.

Write down two things:

  1. What's on it – 3 to 5 short points
  2. How it looks – one sentence
Example: a page for a group presentation • Title: "Maternal and Child Health"
• A short intro – why the topic matters
• 3 key facts
• Our group members
• Sources
Look: calm, light colours, easy to read on a phone.
🧰

Get a writing tool for codeabout 10 minutes

›

You need a simple program to save the code. Word or Pages won't work – they secretly add extra formatting.

  1. Search for "Visual Studio Code" and download it (free)
  2. Install it and open it
Think of it like… a very plain notebook. It saves exactly what you paste – nothing more.
💬

Ask the AI to build itabout 10 minutes

›

Open an AI chat (for example Claude or ChatGPT) and send a message like this. Fill in the gaps with your plan from step 1:

Please create a simple webpage as one single index.html file.

It's about: your topic
It should show: your 3–5 points
It should look: your style sentence

It must work on phones and laptops.
Not happy with the result? Just chat with it: "Make the title bigger", "Use green instead of blue", "Add a section for sources." You'll get an updated version.
Be careful Anyone can see your page. Don't put private things on it – no home address, passwords or patient information.
💾

Save it on your computerabout 5 minutes

›
  1. Make a new folder on your Desktop, e.g. my-website
  2. In Visual Studio Code: File → New File
  3. Paste everything the AI gave you
  4. File → Save As → pick your folder → call it index.html
📁 my-website → 📄 index.html
Why this exact name? "index.html" is like the front door of a website. The internet always looks for this name first.
👀

Try it outabout 5 minutes

›

Open your folder and double-click index.html. Your page opens in the browser! 🎉

Good to know Right now only you can see it – it's still just on your laptop. Part 2 puts it on the internet.
Something looks off? Tell the AI what you see ("the text is too small on my phone"), paste the new code, save, and open the file again.
Part 2 · Put it online
👤

Sign up at GitHubabout 5 minutes

›

GitHub is a free website that can show your page to the world. Think of it as free online storage that turns your file into a real website.

  1. Go to github.com and click Sign up
  2. Pick a username – it becomes part of your web address
  3. Confirm your email
Example If your username is anna-health, your link will start with anna-health.github.io
📤

Upload your fileabout 10 minutes

›

First you make an online folder (GitHub calls it a repository), then you drop your file in.

  1. Click the + at the top right → New repository
  2. Give it a name, e.g. my-website
  3. Choose Public
  4. Click Create repository
  5. Click the link uploading an existing file
  6. Drag your index.html into the window
  7. Click the green Commit changes button
"Commit changes"? That's just GitHub's way of saying "Save".
🌍

Switch on your websiteabout 5 minutes

›
  1. In your online folder, click Settings
  2. On the left, click Pages
  3. Under "Branch", choose main and click Save
  4. Get a coffee ☕ – it takes 1–3 minutes
  5. Reload the page. You'll see "Your site is live" and your link
anna-health.github.io/my-website
It says "404" or "not found"? Wait a few more minutes. Still not working? Check that your file is called exactly index.html.
Optional extra: show your link on GitHub Go back to the main page of your online folder. On the right, next to About, click the little ⚙️ gear. Tick "Use your GitHub Pages website" and click Save changes.
This doesn't change your website – it just shows your link on GitHub, so visitors can click it right away.
🎉

Share it – and change it any timewhenever you like

›

Share your link with classmates, put it on a slide, or turn it into a QR code.

Want to change something later?

  1. Ask the AI for the change and save the new file
  2. In your online folder: Add file → Upload files
  3. Drop in the new index.html and click Commit changes

A minute later, your website shows the new version.

🥳

You did it!

Your webpage is online. Plan → ask the AI → save → upload → share. That's all there is to it.
